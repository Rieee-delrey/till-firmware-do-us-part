One wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding: funny and heartfelt in a single sentence. A model judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the vow with the bigger laugh and the warmer heart.

Entry (104 characters):

> I take you to be my favourite human, and I promise never to update my firmware without asking you first.

`main.py` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, reads it back to verify the bytes, and asserts the 1–200 character gate and exactly one sentence.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Till Firmware Do Us Part" ($2.50, top 10).
